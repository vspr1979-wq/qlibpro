Do NOT hardcode fixed trading rules, indicator thresholds, strike-selection rules, or a manually designed BUY/SELL scoring formula.

Feed live Upstox underlying + option-chain data and historical data into Microsoft Qlib. Let Qlib perform quantitative feature generation, factor analysis, model training/evaluation and strategy discovery to identify which option contracts and market conditions have predictive value.

The application should dynamically determine:

CE vs PE
strike selection within the available option chain
normal-premium vs small-premium opportunity
entry/target/stop levels
expected movement
position quantity subject to risk/capital limits
BUY CE / BUY PE / NO TRADE

The application should not assume ATM, cheapest premium, highest volume, or any fixed indicator threshold.

The strategy engine should use Qlib's quantitative output together with live market conditions and risk constraints. If Qlib/model confidence and market evidence are insufficient, return NO TRADE.

The system should continuously learn/retrain from historical and newly collected market data and evaluate strategies using out-of-sample/backtesting validation before using them for live signals.
